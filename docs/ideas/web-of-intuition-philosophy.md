# The Web of Intuition (Philosophy)

## I. The Nature of the Table Spread
In the traditional Zettelkasten, the "table spread" is an ephemeral moment of discovery. The Semantic Hologram transforms this into a preserved cognitive artifact. By saving the path of intuition as a child hologram, we preserve not just the conclusion, but the journey.

## II. Sovereignty vs. Standardization
We reject the "universal schema." Meaning is idiosyncratic. True collaboration occurs not when we are forced into a common language, but when we build translation layers between our personal ontologies. The `.data.json` manifest is the bridge between two different minds.

## III. Inhabiting Thought
Collaboration is not the exchange of static files; it is the act of inhabiting a shared world-space. By sharing camera positions and cursor attention, we move from "looking at a graph" to "walking through a thought process together."

## IV. The Club Model of Knowledge
Knowledge should not be a binary of "private" or "public." We envision a tiered network of "clubs"—spaces where intellectual luminaries can jam in high-signal, protected environments, while their final insights propagate back to the public web as open-source cognitive maps.

## V. A Return to Web 1.0
We embrace the spirit of the early web: decentralized, static-file based, and sovereign. By using Git and Tor, we remove the corporate gatekeeper and return the power of knowledge organization to the individual, while using NATS and libp2p to provide the live connectivity of the modern era.
